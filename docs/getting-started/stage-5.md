# Stage 5 · Architect

**For:** enterprise architects and technology leaders (roughly the 20-year level).
**Time:** about 4 weeks.

Technology is the smaller part of this stage. The work is deciding under
uncertainty, writing the decision down, and living with it for years.

## Reading order

1. [5.1 Cloud, Exadata and Autonomous](../cloud-database/01-cloud.md)
2. [5.2 Global and multi-region architecture](../enterprise-architecture/01-global-architecture.md)
3. [5.3 Strategy, governance and ADRs](../enterprise-architecture/02-strategy-governance.md)

## Exit test

- [ ] Write an architecture decision record for a real system, including the options you rejected.
- [ ] Present a five-year database roadmap to a non-technical audience in ten minutes.
- [ ] Defend a platform standard against a team that wants an exception, and say when you would grant it.
- [ ] Answer the [20-years bank](../interview-preparation/05-level-20.md) aloud.
